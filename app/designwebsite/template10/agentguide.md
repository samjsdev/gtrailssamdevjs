# Template 10: Haute Couture Architecture & Construction

Inspired by the ultra-luxury, editorial, and monolithic architectural design of **Aparna Kaushik** (`aparnakaushik.com`), tailored specifically for high-end architecture and turnkey construction practices.

## Design Highlights
- **Palette**: Deep Charcoal/Obsidian (`#111111`), Alabaster/Limestone (`#faf8f5`, `#f5f2ea`), Muted Champagne Bronze (`#c5a47e`, `#b89568`), and delicate stone dividers (`#141414`/10).
- **Typography**: `Cinzel` (monumental display serif) + `Plus_Jakarta_Sans` (crisp modernist body with wide tracking `tracking-[0.22em]`) + `Cormorant_Garamond` (editorial italic serif quotes).
- **Signature Sections**:
  1. Monolithic Cinematic Hero with accreditation badges and dynamic client tagline.
  2. Architectural Metrics Ribbon (HeroStats: 300+ Residences, 18+ Accolades, 10-Yr Structural RCC Warranty, 100% Turnkey Delivery).
  3. Editorial Brand Story & Atelier Ethos spread ("Designing a home is like drawing a portrait of your client").
  4. Principal Architect & Leadership Monograph with credentials.
  5. Interactive Disciplines & Services showcase (Iconic Architecture, Turnkey Civil RCC, Haute Interiors, Biophilic Landscapes, Structural Engineering, CMDA Sanctions).
  6. Curated Works Portfolio Gallery with category filtering and interactive project monograph modal.
  7. Construction-to-Masterpiece Before & After split slider (bare RCC frame vs completed luxury villa).
  8. Press & Media Insights marquee (AD, Forbes, Vogue, Elle Decor).
  9. Patron Testimonials from verified residential clients.
  10. Monumental Call To Action banner.
  11. Comprehensive subpages: `/about`, `/services`, `/gallery`, `/contact`.

## Repo Law: Separation of Templates and Builds
- The `/build` directory and its contents are generated from templates (in `app/designwebsite/`) combined with client data.
- Template Isolation: Any updates made to this template MUST NOT affect any generated build.
